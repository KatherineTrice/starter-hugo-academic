summary: A poster on the relationship of visual statistical learning and reading in Deaf individuals.
abstract: 'Statistical learning refers to the  implicit learning process to extract regularities from sensory inputs, and shapes and is shaped in turn by language development. However, given the difficulties Deaf individuals face in reading acquisition, and differences in early linguistic experience, their statistical learning abilities and its interrelationship with written language skill may differ. How do deafness, sign language experience, and reading ability intertwine with statistical learning? Find out in our poster!'
